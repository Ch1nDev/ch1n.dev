02 · What gets collected

Only what's needed to work.

The Cloudlight Global Account is the one place where real account data is collected. When you create an account, a few things are needed to make it function — and nothing beyond that.

🪪
Identity
Your chosen username, display name, and email address. The email is used for login and account recovery only — not marketing, not sharing with anyone.
🔒
Password
Stored as a one-way hash — it cannot be reversed. I cannot see your password. Nobody can.
🕐
Login metadata
Account creation date, last login timestamp, and IP address at login. The IP is held for a limited time for security — detecting unauthorised access and enforcing bans where legally required.
💳
Payments — not handled by us
If a paid Pro tier is introduced, payment goes through a third-party processor like Stripe. No card details pass through our systems — we receive only a transaction confirmation.

"I collect the minimum needed to make the software work. Nothing more gets added without updating this policy first."

03 · Why it's collected

Four reasons. No others.

Every piece of data collected serves one of these purposes. If it doesn't fit one of them, it shouldn't be collected.

Your email and password hash let you log in. That's what they're for.
🛡️

Login metadata helps detect unauthorised access and supports the global ban system for serious violations across the federated network.

We do not build advertising profiles on Cloudlight account data. We do not sell personal data. We do not share it with third parties for any commercial purpose. We do not use your account data for anything beyond operating the service.

04 · Cookies & advertising

Cookies: honest about what's there.

Some sites under this umbrella use cookies — small text files stored in your browser. This section explains exactly which sites use them, what for, and who controls them. You are always asked before optional cookies are set.

⚖️ Legal basis — UK PECR & UK GDPR

Because chinberclart.dev operates from the UK, the relevant laws are the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) and UK GDPR. Under PECR, non-essential cookies require your prior, freely given, informed consent. That consent is collected via the cookie banner on each site before any optional cookies are placed. You can withdraw consent at any time by reopening cookie settings.

Google Consent Mode v2.

Both chin web and BTFLY.SOCIAL use Google AdSense to display advertisements. To comply with UK and EU privacy law, these sites implement Google Consent Mode v2 — a framework that signals your consent choices directly to Google before any ad code runs.

By default, when you first visit, everything is denied. Google AdSense fires in a limited, non-personalised mode only. If you accept advertising cookies via the banner, Google may serve personalised ads based on your broader browsing history. If you decline, ads may still appear but they will not be targeted to you personally.

05 · chin web specifics

Search stays in your browser.

chin web is a search frontend that passes your queries to the Brave Search API via a Cloudflare Worker proxy. Here's exactly what happens with your data at each step:

🔍
Your search query
Sent from your browser to our Cloudflare Worker at api.search.chinberclart.dev, which forwards it to Brave Search. The Worker is configured to keep the API key server-side — it is never exposed to your browser. Brave Search's own privacy policy applies to how they handle queries.
📜
Search history
If you enable history via the cookie banner, your recent searches are stored in a cookie in your browser only. They are never sent to our servers or to Brave. You can clear them at any time from the History panel, or by clearing your browser cookies.
📢
Advertising
Google AdSense is active on chin web. If you accept advertising cookies, Google may use your broader browsing profile to personalise ads. If you decline, AdSense serves non-personalised ads only. No search query data is shared with Google by us — any Google cookie activity relates to the ad unit only.
🚫
What chin web does not collect
We do not log your search queries server-side. We do not store your IP beyond what Cloudflare retains for infrastructure purposes. We do not build any profile of your search behaviour.
06 · BTFLY.SOCIAL specifics

Federation means data lives in multiple places.

BTFLY is federated — posts can live on independently operated node servers run by third parties. This changes how data works in ways worth being clear about upfront.

🌐
Global identity vs. node account
Your Cloudlight Global account is held centrally and covered by this policy. Your node-level profiles and posts are held by whoever operates that node — under their own applicable law and policy.
💡
Light servers replicate — by design
Posts on Light servers are distributed across the network and persist even after the originating server shuts down. Once replicated, content may exist on servers outside our control. Post on Light servers with this in mind.
☁️
Grey and Dark servers stay local
Grey server content lives only on that server — if it goes offline, the posts go with it. Dark servers are fully isolated and not discoverable on the global network.
📢
Advertising on BTFLY.SOCIAL
Google AdSense is currently active on the BTFLY landing page (btfly.social). The same Consent Mode v2 system applies as described in the cookies section above. When the platform launches, any in-app advertising will be described in a policy update before going live.
💾
Pro cloud backup
If you subscribe to Pro and enable cloud backup, copies of your posts are stored centrally — accessible only by you, not shared with anyone.
07 · CLOUDLIGHT.GAMES specifics

Games, saves, and leaderboards.

CLOUDLIGHT.GAMES connects to the Cloudlight account for optional features. Nothing is stored without a reason, and nothing optional is stored without opting in.

🎮 How game data works

Cloud saves: If a game supports cloud saves and you opt in, save data is stored against your Cloudlight account — accessible only by you.

Leaderboards: If a game has a public leaderboard, your display name and score will be visible to other players. You can always choose not to submit.

Analytics: If gameplay analytics are collected, this is disclosed per-game. No behavioural or personal data is collected without clear disclosure first.

Third-party stores: If games are distributed on Steam, itch.io, or similar platforms, those platforms' own policies apply to that distribution channel.
